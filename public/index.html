CritCall

Routes a critical lab result to the clinician covering the patient right now, not just the one who ordered the test. Contact in one click, closed-loop read-back, and escalation before the 30-minute limit.

Demo only. All patients, clinicians and schedules are fictional.

Lab tech

Critical result queue, who is covering, one-click notify, read-back and audit trail.

Clinician phone

The alert as the covering clinician sees it. Acknowledge with read-back.

Supervisor

Open results by time left, escalations, time-to-ack and breaches.

Demo script

Open Lab tech and Clinician phone side by side. Use Reset demo between runs.

Scenario A: the ordering provider is off shift
  1. Click Post critical result: A. John Doe's potassium is 6.8. Dr. Patel ordered it at 07:10.
  2. CritCall shows Covering now: Dr. Linh Nguyen and warns that Dr. Patel has been off shift since 19:00.
  3. Click Notify covering clinician. On the phone (Dr. Nguyen), read back "6.8" and tap Acknowledge.
  4. Back in the lab view, click Verify read-back and close. The audit timeline shows the full loop.
Scenario B: no answer, so escalation runs
  1. Post B and notify Dr. Kim. Do not acknowledge.
  2. Click +10 min. Dr. Okafor (backup) is alerted automatically. Switch the phone to Dr. Okafor and acknowledge.
  3. Optional: keep fast-forwarding instead, to show the charge nurse alert at 20 min and the red flag at 25.
Scenario C: the procedure team is covering
  1. Post C. Rosa Garcia is in the cath lab, so CritCall routes to the cardiology fellow, Dr. Shah, instead of her primary team.
